# Dework Docs

## Dework Docs

- [What is Dework?](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/what-is-dework.md): Get to know about Dework as a tool for DAOs and web3 projects!
- [Getting started on Dework](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/getting-started-on-dework.md): Based on who you are there are two ways to start using the app
- [Setup your first Space](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/setup-your-first-space.md): This will help you understand how to utilise the key Dework features
- [Nested Workspaces](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/setup-your-first-space/nested-workspaces.md): Nest Spaces and Projects under an existing Space for finer control over your tasks
- [Activity Feed](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/setup-your-first-space/activity-feed.md): Provide updates to your community/team, via Discord as well
- [Permissions](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/setup-your-first-space/permissions.md): Flexibly set various permissions that match your needs
- [Creating your first bounty](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/creating-your-first-bounty.md): Task + Reward = Bounty
- [Connecting to Discord](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/connecting-to-discord.md): Keep everyone up to date on your project's progress
- [Featuring a Role](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/featuring-a-role.md): You can feature roles to let contributors directly apply to them
- [Manage Payments](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/manage-payments.md): Utilise various options to pay a reward on Dework
- [Single Payment](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/manage-payments/single-payment.md): Pay rewards for individual bounties using supported wallets
- [Batch Payments](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-orgs/manage-payments/batch-payments.md): Simplify your contributor payments, or just save on transaction fees
- [Browsing DAOs on Dework](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-contributors/browsing-daos-on-dework.md): Find opportunities and communities to connect with
- [Exploring Bounties](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-contributors/exploring-bounties.md): Browse the list of open bounties from DAOs and projects across Dework
- [Task Feed](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-contributors/task-feed.md): Easily discover tasks that match your skills from DAOs you follow
- [Reputation Staking](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/guides-for-contributors/reputation-staking.md): Reputation Staking allows highly skilled contributors to quickly stand-out among applicants and make the client-contractor matching process easier.
- [Your Inbox](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/your-inbox.md): Stay up-to-date on your Dework tasks
- [Payments](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/payments.md): Ensure that you're setup to make or accept payments
- [Task Types & Assignee Gating](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/task-types-and-assignee-gating.md): There are various types of task types you can use in Dework to fit your team or community
- [Task Views](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/task-views.md): Edit & create new views to aid discovery, collaboration & action
- [Org Community Suggestions](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/org-community-suggestions.md): Community Suggestions aggregated at the organisation level
- [Task Templates](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/task-templates.md): Make templates that you can reuse when creating new tasks
- [Project Roadmap](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/project-roadmap.md): View all your ongoing projects on a timeline
- [Roles Center](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/roles-center.md): Feature specific Discord roles and let contributors apply to them
- [Bookmarks](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/bookmarks.md): Use bookmarks to save tasks you want to review or engage with later
- [My Task Board](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/my-task-board.md)
- [Wallet Connect](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/fundamentals/wallet-connect.md): Sign in to Dework on your mobile with your supported wallet app
- [GitHub](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/integrations/github.md): Two-way sync for your developers to be connected with your team and community
- [Discord](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/integrations/discord.md): Integrate with Discord to engage your server with Task Awareness, Discussions, Community Suggestions and more!
- [Coordinape](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/integrations/coordinape.md): Show Dework tasks on user profiles within your Coordinape circle
- [Notion](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/integrations/notion.md): Create bounties out of Notion tasks in 1-click
- [Tasks](https://dework.gitbook.io/product-docs/workflows/tasks.md)
