TLDR;🗺 Roadmap - a timeline for your projects🧬 Multi-token bounties🗺 RoadmapThe Roadmap makes it possible to create a high-level timeline of your organisation’s projects. To enable the Roadmap, go to your organisation’s settings and under “General”, click “Enable Project Roadmap”.🧬 Multi-token bountiesYou can now create multi-token bounties. This way, you can create bounties that are e.g 80% USDC and 20% your native DAO token.
