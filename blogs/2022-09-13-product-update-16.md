New this week:🔄 Notion Bounty Sync🖼 Improved in-app Notifications🔄 Notion Bounty SyncYou can now connect a Dework Space to a Notion board in order to sync Bounties! Doing so will automatically add a “Setup bounty” button to your Notion tasks and you will be able to use that button to very easily create Bounties out of these tasks. Once a bounty has been created in Dework, its amount and details will also be reflected in Notion.
